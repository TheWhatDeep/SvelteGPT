How to Play
Survive. Upgrade. Repeat.

Move — drag anywhere on the left half of the screen. The stick appears under your thumb.

Shoot — drag on the right half. You fire in the direction you point, and only while you are pointing. Two thumbs, both busy.

Auto-aim — off by default. Turn it on from the menu or the pause screen and your weapon tracks the nearest walker on its own, so one thumb is enough. Your own aim always overrides it.

Pick up a weapon — white crates turn up ahead of you after each horde. A white arrow at the screen edge points to one that is off-screen. Walk over it to swap.

The Director — decides when the dead come. They trickle in, build to a horde, then give you a breather once you have caught your breath — not on a clock. Survive a horde and choose one of three permanent upgrades; they stack for the whole run. Supplies you are short of turn up ahead of where you are heading.

The dead, in order of appearance

Shambler — green, slow, arrives in numbers.
Runner — pale and fast, closes before you notice.
Crawler — tan, scuttles on all fours, low and easy to lose.
Brute — grey and huge, soaks a magazine.
Spitter — ochre, hangs back and lobs acid.
Bloater — swollen and yellow-green; it detonates when it dies, so do not kill it at arm's length.
Howler — red-brown; its shriek sends every walker nearby into a sprint. Shoot it first.

Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to aim, hold the button to fire.
